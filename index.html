Where Asure Intelligence goes next.

Not a list of promises. A programme with gates you can check — and a knowledge layer underneath that everything else plugs into.

The programme

Four quarters, each with a gate you can check.

  1. Q1M1–3

    Foundation

    Standards pack approved, meeting capture live, AI policy signed, and two pilots measured against real baselines.

    Gate Flagship BIM pilot run against P285 Tower 3 GFC + BOQ — BOQ drafter and Uniclass matcher in assisted mode.

  2. Q2M4–6

    Workflows

    The mapped ways of working move from documented to actually used, with context files live across teams and the first small agents appearing.

    Gate At least 70% of the 72 mapped ways used once per team; first ten agents running.

  3. Q3M7–9

    Agents

    Agents carry daily production work with a named human verifier on anything generative, and confidential work routes to models running on our own hardware.

    Gate Five or more agents in daily use, first-pass acceptance at 60% or better, spend inside budget, local route live.

  4. Q4M10–12

    The Loop

    The studio can ask its own knowledge a question and get an answer with its source attached — and the numbers come back to the people steering.

    Gate Principal dashboards live, “Ask ADS” v1 on the knowledge server, KPI review against goals.

  5. Y2M12+

    Year-two decisions

    Deliberately undecided. A fine-tuned studio model, surrogate simulation and productisation are all go/no-go calls that need a year of evidence first.

    Held back Nothing here is committed — each needs the first year’s corpus and a hardware check before it is even scoped.

The layer underneath

Everything the studio knows, in one place you can ask.

Scattered sources become one retrievable layer — scoped by role and project, and every answer carries where it came from.

How to read this Quarters are gates, not delivery dates — each is a check that either passes or does not. Year-two items are listed precisely because they are undecided.
