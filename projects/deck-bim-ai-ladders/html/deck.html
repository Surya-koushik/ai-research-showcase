Why

Why BIM and AI — and why now

Our models hold the studio’s knowledge; AI is how that knowledge compounds instead of walking out the door. Richer models make smarter AI — smarter AI makes richer models effortless.

The cost of standing still

Drawings redrawn, quantities re-counted, clashes found on site, the same coordination call every week. Skill lives in heads; information lives in flat files. When someone leaves, the loop resets.

The opportunity

Put the knowledge in the model, and let AI run the loop — coordinated, checked, consistent. Time shifts from redoing to designing and deciding.

What we’re doing

What this is — the outline

No dates, no Gantt chart. Two ladders every person and team can locate themselves on — and one loop that makes the whole studio smarter with every project.

  • Two ladders, not a deadline. Ten levels of BIM maturity and ten stages of AI — from a 2D line and a chat window to a self-running studio.
  • Every rung shows Use + Results. What you do at that level, and the payoff you get for reaching it.
  • Our tools, mapped on. Where ADS already sits on each ladder — and it’s higher than most.
  • One honest self-check. Where are you on each ladder, really? Then take one rung.

01
Draw · Entry

2D CAD drafting

Lines, arcs and text on named layers. The drawing is the deliverable and the computer can’t tell a wall from a wardrobe. Coordination happens by eye, across separate files.

At ADS: AutoCAD DWG sets on the ADS ten-layer standard.
Use
Produce plans, sections & details as flat 2D sheets
Results
Fast to start — but every change is manual and rework is high
Deliverable
Coordinated-by-hand DWG / PDF drawing set

Draw

3D modelling — geometry only

You build the shape in 3D to see it and sell it — massing, form, renders — but the model is dumb geometry with no data, and drawings are still made separately.

At ADS: SketchUp / Rhino massing, ArchViz AI visuals.
Use

Deliverable

Why the two ladders are really one

BIM and AI climb together

These aren’t two projects competing for time. Each ladder pulls the other up — that’s why we run them side by side.

BIM makes AI possible

Data-rich models (Level 4 and up) are the fuel: structured information AI can query, check and generate against. No good model, no good AI — garbage in, garbage out.

AI makes BIM effortless

Agents and generative tools carry you up the BIM ladder — clash reports, quantities, options, self-updating twins — work that used to take weeks now runs in the background.

Every rung of BIM you gain, AI can automate; every stage of AI you reach, your BIM gets richer. Two ladders, one studio, one loop.

How every rung is actually built

Close the loop — the four points

This is how the best AI companies compound: not one big leap, but a loop that turns everyday work into a system that keeps improving. Same loop, for a design studio — on both ladders.

01

Capture

Do the task — and record what you actually did, step by step, while you do it. Real work, not theory.

→
02

Codify

Turn it into a written procedure or a modelled standard: the steps, the inputs, the tools, the data.

→
03

Delegate

Hand it to AI — a prompt, an agent, a tool. Now the studio can run it, not just you.

→
04

Compound

Every run feeds the studio brain and frees your time. It gets faster, better — then back to Capture.

Your action item

Take one rung on each ladder — this week

You don’t need to build a twin or an agent. You need one concrete step on each ladder, small enough to finish this week and real enough to compound.

Level up one BIM object
  1. Pick a family or a standard detail
  2. Add the data: materials, spec, IDs
  3. Make it schedule-ready
  4. Save it to the ADS library
Federate one project
  1. Combine arch + struct + MEP
  2. Run one clash test
  3. Log the top conflicts
  4. Share the coordination report
Document one task for AI
  1. Write how you do one real thing
  2. List steps, inputs, tools, suppliers
  3. Hand it to a prompt or agent
  4. Let it run it next time
